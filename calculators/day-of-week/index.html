How to use it

  1. Choose one Gregorian date.
  2. Read or copy the English weekday name.

Method and limitations

  • The input must be a real YYYY-MM-DD date from 0001 through 9999.
  • The date is constructed at UTC midnight and read with the UTC weekday rule, so the browser's time zone cannot move it to another weekday.
  • The proleptic Gregorian calendar is used for all supported years. Historical local calendar adoption is not reconstructed.

Examples

29 Feb 2024: Thursday.

1 Jan 2000: Saturday.
