How to use it

  1. Choose one start date.
  2. Enter a signed whole-day offset: positive to add, negative to subtract or zero to keep the date.
  3. Read or copy the resulting date.

Method and limitations

  • The start must be a real YYYY-MM-DD Gregorian date and the offset must be a whole number.
  • Arithmetic uses UTC date-only boundaries of 86,400,000 milliseconds. Daylight-saving transitions and the browser's time zone cannot change the result.
  • Only calendar days are moved. Business-day, holiday, local deadline and time-of-day rules are not applied.
  • Results are limited to dates from 0001-01-01 through 9999-12-31.

Examples

28 Feb 2024 + 1 day: 29 Feb 2024.

1 Jan 2026 − 1 day: 31 Dec 2025.
