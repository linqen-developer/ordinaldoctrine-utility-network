Business-day count

Choose a start date and an end date to count business days.

Related tools

  • Date difference

  • Add or subtract date

How to use it

  1. Choose a start date and an end date. Reverse order is valid.
  2. Choose whether the entered start date and entered end date are included.
  3. Read or copy the Monday-to-Friday count.

Method and limitations

  • Monday through Friday count as business days; Saturday and Sunday do not.
  • The start date is excluded and the end date is included by default. Each checkbox applies to the endpoint named on the input, even in reverse order.
  • If both inputs are the same weekday, it is counted once when either endpoint is included and zero times when both are excluded.
  • Public holidays, observances, country rules, alternate workweeks and legal deadline rules are not included.

Examples

Mon 5 Oct 2026 → Mon 12 Oct 2026, start excluded and end included: 5 business days.

Sat 10 Oct 2026 → Mon 12 Oct 2026: 1 business day with the default endpoint rules.
