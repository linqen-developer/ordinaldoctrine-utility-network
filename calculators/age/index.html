How to use it

  1. Choose a date of birth.
  2. Choose the date on which to measure age.
  3. Read or copy the completed years, remaining months and remaining days.

Method and limitations

  • Both inputs must be real YYYY-MM-DD Gregorian dates. The selected date cannot be before the date of birth.
  • The result uses the common birthday-based system: age increases after a completed birthday, not at the start of a calendar year.
  • If a birthday day does not exist in a target month, it is clamped to that month's last day. For example, 29 February reaches a yearly anniversary on 28 February in a non-leap year, and 31 January plus one completed month reaches the last day of February.
  • No cultural, legal, gestational or time-of-day age convention is applied.

Examples

29 Feb 2024 → 28 Feb 2025: 1 year, 0 months and 0 days under the stated month-end rule.

15 Jun 2000 → 20 Aug 2026: 26 years, 2 months and 5 days.
