How to use it

  1. Choose one Gregorian date.
  2. Read or copy its ISO week-year and week number.

Method and limitations

  • ISO 8601 weeks start on Monday.
  • Week 1 is the week containing 4 January, equivalently the first Thursday of the ISO week-year.
  • The ISO week-year can differ from the date's calendar year near New Year. No locale-specific Sunday-first or partial-week rule is used.

Examples

1 Jan 2016: 2015-W53.

4 Jan 2016: 2016-W01.
