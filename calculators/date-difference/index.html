How to use it

  1. Choose a start date and an end date.
  2. Optionally count the start date as day 1.
  3. Read or copy the automatic result. Reverse order is valid.

How it works

  • Each value is validated as a real YYYY-MM-DD calendar date.
  • Dates are compared at UTC midnight in whole 86,400,000 millisecond days, so daylight-saving changes do not alter the count.
  • The ordinary difference excludes the start date. The option adds one counted day without changing the difference.
  • Time of day, time zone, holidays, business days and legal deadlines are not used.

Examples

4 Oct 2026 → 3 Nov 2026: 30 calendar days, or 4 weeks and 2 days.

29 Feb 2024 → 1 Mar 2024: 1 calendar day.
