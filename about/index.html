About these utilities

Ordinal Doctrine is a small collection of calm, single-purpose utilities.

What we build

The current tools compare calendar dates, count text and convert common length units. Each one puts the working control first, names its assumptions and keeps explanatory content below the result.

How the tools work

Calculations run in browser-native JavaScript. The site uses system fonts and its own small static files. It has no account system, advertising, analytics or third-party interface assets.

What we do not claim

These tools are general utilities, not official, legal or professional services. They do not calculate holidays, business deadlines or rules that vary by jurisdiction.
