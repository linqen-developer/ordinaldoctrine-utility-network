Method & limits
  • Processes one static JPEG, PNG or WebP locally. 1 KB = 1,000 bytes; 1 MB = 1,000,000 bytes.
  • An original already below the limit is returned unchanged. Otherwise quality and then dimensions may be reduced.
  • Maximum input: 50 MB, 50 MP and 16,384 px per axis. Re-encoding is limited to 12 MP.
  • Animation and unsupported formats are rejected. Re-encoding can remove metadata or color-profile information; file details stay available before download.
  • Verified on current Windows Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Safari, iOS and physical mobile devices are not claimed as tested.
